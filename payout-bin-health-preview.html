BIN CHECKUP

Receiving card BINs whose Push-to-Card delivery rate suddenly broke against a healthy baseline, caught at onset by the daily tiers.

▶ How to read this board what every number means

This board watches Push-to-Card payout delivery at the receiving card-BIN grain — a single card BIN going bad on an otherwise-healthy destination surfaces on its own (a partner/destination monitor would never see it). Each card is one receiving country × card BIN. A BIN appears only when its delivery rate breaks against its own healthy baseline, and drops off automatically once its most recent 2 days recover above 85%.

The two pages

Critical — a near-total break right now: the most recent 2 days are ≤ 10% delivery.
Warn — flagged, but not a current near-total break — the recent 2-day rate is above 10% (a partial break, or a BIN on the mend). A BIN whose most recent 2 days fully recover above 85% drops off the board entirely.

The big number & sorting

The switch under the tabs flips the big number between Worst rate — delivery over the most recent 2 days, the current state — and Undelivered — the count of transfers that didn't complete (sent − delivered) over the last 7 days. Either way the list is ranked by undelivered transfers within each page, Critical first.

Delivery rate — four lenses (healthy → onset → now)

Baseline — the BIN's healthy rate: median weekly delivery over the prior 8 weeks. The flag fires against this.
First dip — delivery rate over the first 2 days of the break (starting at the “First dip” date). A snapshot of onset, not the current state — shown for every BIN, including thin ones caught by Sustained.
7-day — delivery over the most recent 7 days (current sustained state). Shown n/a until the break is 7 days old — before that the window blends in healthy pre-break days and understates the outage. (A BIN can be ongoing yet younger than 7 days: “ongoing” means ≥3 breaching days, not a 7-day-old break.)
2-day — delivery over the most recent 2 days (the sharpest read of right now). Critical is keyed to this.

Baseline and First dip are anchored at the break's onset; 7-day and 2-day are trailing windows anchored to now (maturity-guarded, so the partial latest day is excluded). Tap any of the First dip / 7-day / 2-day chips to see the sent & undelivered counts for that window. Colored green ≥85%, amber 50–85%, red <50%.

The bar — delivered vs undelivered since the first dip

Splits every transfer from the first-dip date through now into delivered and undelivered — the whole break (its length in days is in the label), not a fixed trailing window.

Error breakdown

The three chips are this BIN’s top transaction error codes among its undelivered transfers (last 14 days), each with its share. “No error code” = ended undelivered with nothing recorded (typically a customer cancellation, or still pending / overdue). Tap a chip for the 3 most recent sample transactions with that code — each is a COFFEE reference number — click it to open that transfer in COFFEE. Per-window sent/undelivered counts live in the First dip / 7-day / 2-day chip popovers.

Onset vs ongoing

An onset break was caught by the acute 2-day check only — it just started dropping (the fast, early signal, under 7 days old). An ongoing break also trips the sustained 7-day check, meaning it's been failing across multiple days, not a one-off.

How a BIN gets flagged — the tiers

AcuteThe first 2-day window (≥10 sent) whose rate falls ≥40pp below baseline — the onset signal, shown on each card as “first dip.” Catches a break the same/next day. Note this is the first 2 days of the break, not the most recent 2 (that's the “2-day” lens).
Sustained≥3 breaching days in the last 7 (day sent ≥3 & rate <50%), 7-day sent ≥15 and rate <50% — a multi-day sag that marks a break as ongoing.

Baseline healthy gate B ≥ 90%, prior-8-week sent ≥ 80. The most recent (partial) day is dropped — a maturity guard, since Push-to-Card settles fast but not instantly.
