DELIVERY WATCH

Payout routes where transfers are failing to reach recipients, ranked by how many deliveries were lost in the last 5 days.

▶ How to read this board what every number means

This board watches payout delivery — whether the money a customer sent actually reaches the recipient. Each card is one route: a receiving bank/wallet (the destination), the partner that delivers to it, and the method (bank deposit, cash pickup, home delivery). A route appears here only when its delivery is degraded. All counts are the last 5 days; all times are UTC.

The two pages

Ongoing — still failing right now (delivery over the last 2 days is under 50%).
Recovering — bouncing back (last-2-day delivery is 50–85%, not yet fully healthy).

The big number — Lost transfers

Lost transfers = undelivered transfers in the last 5 days that are either failed (cancelled or terminated — they will never arrive) or overdue (past their promised delivery time). It is a count of real customer transfers that did not get delivered, and it's what the list is ranked by within each page (also called Impact). Transfers still moving within their promised window are not counted — that's normal settlement lag, not a failure.

Delivery rate — three moments

Normal — the route's healthy delivery rate 30–90 days ago (before the drop).
Since the dip — the delivery rate across this route's dip window, from when it dropped through now (or through recovery).
Now — delivery rate over the last 2 days (the current state).

Colored green ≥85%, amber 50–85%, red <50%.

Slow vs failing — mature delivery rate (delivery% including late)

Mature delivery rate is the delivery rate among transfers that have had a fair chance to land, judged against each route's own normal speed — not a fixed clock or the promised date. A transfer counts once it has concluded: delivered, failed/cancelled, or been pending longer than this route usually takes (its 95th-percentile delivery time, learned from 30–90 days of history). Transfers still moving within that normal time are excluded. We look only at transfers that concluded in the last ~7 days, so it reflects the current state, not a stale historical average. Colored the same green / amber / red as the delivery rates.

This is how we separate a slow destination from a failing one. A route is tagged ⧗ slow — processing lag when its mature delivery rate is 70% or higher: transfers are still landing once they conclude, so the low live rate is just processing lag, not an outage. Trinidad bank deposits, for example, take ~5 days to deliver, so their live rate reads near 0% while transfers are still legitimately in flight — but once those transfers conclude they arrive ~95%+, so the mature delivery rate stays high and the route is tagged slow. A genuinely failing route has a mature delivery rate below 70% even at its own pace. Each card also shows the route's typical processing time (p50 / p90). Untick Show slow destinations to hide the slow ones and keep only real failures. When too few transfers have concluded yet to judge, a route is left in view rather than hidden.

The bar — delivered vs undelivered since the dip

Splits every transfer from the dip's start through now into delivered and undelivered — the whole break (its length in days is in the label), not a fixed trailing window. For recovered routes it covers the dip window itself.

Reroute & Hourly@risk

Reroute — a currently-healthy alternate partner already serving the same destination + method you could shift volume to.
Hourly@risk — the most transfers caught in a single bad hour (how sharp the spike was). "n/a" = too low-volume for the hourly check.

How a route gets flagged — the three checks

HourlyA sharp within-day plunge on a very high-volume destination (≥200 transfers at risk in a consecutive-hour burst).
IncidentOne clearly-bad day (delivery <40% with ≥50 transfers at risk) on a mid-size route, versus its healthy 30–90d baseline.
SustainedA slow multi-day bleed on a smaller route (7-day delivery <50% across ≥3 bad days). Catches low-volume outages the other two miss.

Cash pickup — 3-day maturity window

Cash Pick-up routes run noisier and settle slower than bank deposit, so a fresh dip is often just settlement noise rather than a real outage. A cash-pickup route is therefore held back until its dip has been running at least 3 days — the outage only flags once it's mature. Bank Deposit and Home Delivery flag immediately, as before.

About “⚠ Top failure types.” For every transfer that did not reach the recipient in the last 5 days (final state failed, cancelled, undelivered, or reversed), we take the last partner error code recorded across its disbursement lifecycle — the terminal state itself often carries no code, so we look back for the reason that caused it. Codes are grouped, glossed to plain language, and shown as a share of that route's undelivered transfers. “Undefined” means no error code was recorded in Trino (or live COFFEE) — typically customer cancellations or silent drops. Reasons are at destination × partner grain — matched to the partner actually routing this card (joined on partnercatalog partner_id), so they reflect that partner specifically rather than a blend across the method.
