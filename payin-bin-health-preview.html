Pay-in · card acceptance

BIN HEALTH

Card BINs whose first-attempt acceptance is anomalously low — the customer's card can't fund the transfer. Split by gateway/PSP so a single dead payment route doesn't read as an issuer problem.

▸ how to read this
CRITICAL — accept ≤5%: dead BIN, effectively nothing funds. Dominant decline shows the share — a 2nd reason when the top one is under 69%, a 3rd when the top two are under 80%.
WARN — accept <60% with issuer-restriction the dominant decline.
Suppressed · not BIN — flagged low but the dominant decline is NSF / 3DS / fraud-block / generic: not BIN-health.
Window (90 / 30 / 10d) — trailing window, toggled top-right. The eligibility volume floor scales with it (≈1,000 → ~333 → ~111 first-attempts) so genuinely-dead low-volume BINs still flag in the short windows. The arrow by Accept shows the point change vs the next-larger window (30d vs 90d, 10d vs 30d).
Gateway split — every attempt routes through one gateway/PSP. GATEWAY-ISOLATED: one door dead, others work → route to PSP/routing. GATEWAY-SPLIT: acceptance diverges by door. ALL GATES DOWN: low on every door → genuine BIN health (not one bad route). SINGLE-GATEWAY: only one door has enough volume to judge.
